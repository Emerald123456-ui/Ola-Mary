Walmart Sales Forecasting

Separate ARIMA time-series models for each of Walmart’s 45 stores. I tested stationarity with the ADF test, compared ARIMA orders per store and kept the one with the lowest test-set MAPE, then served 12-week sales forecasts through a FastAPI endpoint.

  • ARIMA
  • Time series
  • MAE / RMSE / MAPE
  • FastAPI
